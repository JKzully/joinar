## Design Context

### Users
Picked serves professional and semi-professional basketball players entering the European player market, plus clubs, managers, coaches and agents searching for available roster fits. The product exists because clubs need players and players need opportunities. The core job is market liquidity: players declare availability, clubs review prospects, interest is exchanged, and contracts can follow.

### Brand Personality
Professional, sharp, market-literate. The interface should feel like European basketball front office work: scouting reports, roster construction, transfer windows, contract conversations and evaluation sheets.

### Aesthetic Direction
White-first, high-end sports editorial with front-office structure: bold type, crisp lines, real European basketball imagery, restrained orange/blue accents, market boards, dossier rows, roster search tables and country cues with flags. Avoid NBA fandom, youth basketball, college recruiting, generic sports marketing, SaaS sections, testimonials, pricing tables, generic FAQs, glassy overlays and decorative badges.

### Design Principles
- Frame Picked as a player market, never just a recruiting platform.
- Use native recruiting language: enter the market, build your dossier, declare availability, review prospects, open roster search.
- Make players and clubs immediately understand the market loop.
- Show basketball context through dossier fields, roster needs, market countries, stats, film and availability.
- Keep profiles draft-first: players enter data, then publish when they are actually available.
